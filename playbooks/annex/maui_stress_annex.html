Maui stress-test annex

Data as of 2026-10-28

Planning aid. Supports, does not replace, county emergency plans. Not an official shelter list.
Stress test, not a forecast. Each test changes one thing in the plan (a site closes, two hazards strike together, a road is cut, more people come) and counts who is affected, from the plan's own data. Deterministic code computes every number from the plan's data; people review the results.

People counted: residents of Census areas whose drive from the area's centre point to the nearest usable 2026 site was 15 minutes or less before the loss and is more than 15 minutes (or no route) after it; areas already beyond 15 minutes are not counted.

174 tests ran for Maui; 115 affect someone or need county data and are listed below. Two-site losses are listed only when they add people beyond either site alone. This is not a statement that the area has no shelter: counties designate and open shelters for each event, and many public buildings are not on the 2026 list.

Questions for counties

These need county knowledge. The plan has no data for them, so it shows no numbers.

  1. Shelter buildings in an earthquake or a strong hurricane: Which shelter buildings have been assessed for earthquake and hurricane-wind resilience, and which need a check before they are used after a strong event?

  3. Cell network outages: Which areas lose mobile phone service first in a long power outage or after wind damage, and how will warnings and shelter information reach them (radio, sirens, door-to-door)?
  4. Schools used as shelters: When a school is used as a shelter, how long can it stay open before classes must restart, and where do residents go next?
  5. Partner capacity: Could each county collect partner capacity (cots, meals, staff, vehicles, interpreters) with a county-run form such as Survey123, with no personal data, so the plan can show pledged capacity instead of 'confirm with partner'?
